Free PDF editor that works in your browser

Open a PDF, change it, and download the result. Editing happens on your device, so your documents are never uploaded. There is no account, no watermark and nothing to install.

What you can do

  • Edit text. Replace a line of existing text, or add new text boxes anywhere.
  • Sign. Draw or type a signature and place it on the page.
  • Mark up. Highlight passages and draw freehand.
  • Redact. Permanently remove sensitive areas from the downloaded file.
  • Add images. Place a PNG or JPEG, such as a logo or a stamp.
  • Organize pages. Merge PDFs, add blank pages, rotate, reorder, duplicate and delete.

How your files are handled

The page is rendered with PDF.js and the new file is written with pdf-lib, both running in this browser tab. Your PDF is read into memory, your edits are kept as a list, and a new PDF is built when you download. The original file is never modified.

Documents are held only in this tab. Closing or refreshing it discards them, so download your work first.

Questions

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?
No. The editor opens, edits and saves PDFs entirely in your browser. Documents are never sent to a server, and the original file on your device is not changed.
Is the PDF editor free?
Yes. There is no account, no watermark and no paid tier.
Can I edit the existing text in a PDF?
Yes, for horizontal, selectable text. The replacement uses a standard PDF font on a white background, so it will not match the original font exactly, and the edited page is flattened to an image with searchable replacement text.
Does redaction really remove the text?
Yes. A redacted page is rebuilt from sanitized pixels, so the original text, images and hidden annotation text on that page are not copied into the downloaded file.
What are the limits?
Up to 50 MB per PDF, 100 MB in total and 200 pages. OCR, password-protected PDFs, form-field editing and certificate-based digital signatures are not supported.
